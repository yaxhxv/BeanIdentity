This is the brand guidelines for Bean Identity, a charac-
ter-driven t-shirt brand that blends bold minimalism 
with playful storytelling. The brand is built on a strong 
umbrella identity supported by five unique bean perso-
nas, each representing a distinct mood and lifestyle. 
These guidelines define the visual language, color sys-
tems, typography, and character usage to ensure con-
sistency across all touchpoints. From apparel design to 
digital presence, this document serves as a foundation 
for creating a cohesive, scalable, and recognizable 
brand experience.BRAND ARCHITECTURE

Primary- Display Font Secondary- Functional Font
TT Ramillas is a high-contrast transitional serif 
typeface that brings elegance and sophistication 
to Bean Identity. With its refined curves, flared 
serifs, and distinctive flame-like details, it creates a 
strong visual presence. Its versatile range of styles 
supports both expressive headlines and polished, 
modern editorial typography across the brand.
Poppins is a clean, geometric sans-serif typeface that 
serves as the functional backbone of Bean Identity. Its 
balanced proportions and modern simplicity ensure 
excellent readability across digital and print applications. 
Versatile and approachable, it complements the 
elegance of TT Ramillas while maintaining clarity in body 
text, UI, and supporting content.Beans
