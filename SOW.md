# BEAN IDENTITY

## Website Development — Scope of Work

```
Platform Shopify (D2C) Version 1.
```
## 01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bean Identity is India's first personality-driven apparel brand built around five distinct character
archetypes - Coffee Bean, Chilli Bean, Vanilla Bean, Jelly Bean, and Green Bean. The current Shopify
website functions as a basic product store but does not reflect the depth, personality, or community-driven
nature of the brand.
This Scope of Work outlines all required revisions to the homepage and supporting pages to ensure the
website becomes a brand experience and not just a transactional storefront. Every change requested
below is tied directly to a specific business or community-building objective.

## 03 DETAILED TASK SPECIFICATIONS

### Task 1: Hero Banner Redesign Ref : https://www.everlane.com/

```
The current hero section has no copy, no headline, and no emotional hook. A first-time visitor from
Instagram has no idea what Bean Identity stands for within 5 seconds. This needs to change immediately.
```
- Full-screen hero banner with brand tagline
- Sub-line below tagline
- Primary CTA button — 'Find Your Bean' — linking directly to the Quiz Page (Task 4)
- Secondary CTA — 'Shop All Beans' — linking to the collections page
- Hero background to use brand-approved visual assets (will be supplied)
- Must be fully responsive — mobile first design
- No auto-playing video in hero — static or subtle animation only
**Priority:** High **Effort:** Medium

### Task 2: Email Capture Pop-Up — Sitewide Ref :https://coppercowcoffee.com/

```
The website currently has zero email/Phone number capture. The pop-up must feel like a community
invitation, not a discount mechanic.
```
- Trigger: Exit-intent on desktop, scroll-depth (60%) on mobile
- Single input field — email address only
- CTA button — 'I'm in'
- Dismiss option — small 'Not now' text link, no X button
- Design must match Bean Identity brand aesthetic
- Do not show the same visitor the pop-up more than once in a 7-day window
- GDPR/data collection compliance note to be added in small text below CTA
**Priority:** High **Effort:** Low-Medium

### Task 3: High Impact copy section Ref :https://coppercowcoffee.com/

- A moving copy section about expressing your identity
- The copy will be provided
**Priority:** High **Effort:** Low-Medium


### Task 4: Scrolling Marquee Band — Homepage

_A scrolling text band that introduces all five beans by name creates immediate visual identity and tells a
first-time visitor that this brand has a universe worth exploring. It also serves as a navigational aid to the
five bean collections._

- Add a horizontally scrolling marquee band on the homepage
- Position: Below the copy section, above Beanagram
- Content: Five bean names scrolling on repeat — 'Coffee Bean · Chilli Bean · Vanilla Bean · Jelly
    Bean · Green Bean · Coffee Bean · Chilli Bean...'
- Each bean name to be clickable — links to respective bean collection page
- Scroll speed: Slow and continuous — not too fast to be unreadable
- Must pause on hover (desktop)
- Must work seamlessly on mobile without layout breaks
**Priority:** Medium **Effort:** Low

### Task 7: Beanagram Section Redesign — Homepage

_The Beanagram section (customer identity stories) is currently the most powerful content on the website
but is buried at the bottom. It needs to move up the page and be redesigned to feel like community stories,
not a standard review widget._

- Relocate Beanagram section — Keep it after marquee band on homepage
- Redesign layout — card-based format, each story in its own styled card
- Each card to show: Customer name, Bean type they identify as (with bean character icon), their
    story (existing copy)
- Add bean character colour accent to each card matching the bean they identify with
- Add 'Share Your Bean Story' CTA button below the section — links to a submission form
- Submission form to collect: Name, Which bean are you, Your story (max 150 words)
- Submitted stories to go into a moderation queue — not auto-published


- Section heading to read: 'The Bean Stories' — not 'Beanagram'
- Minimum 3 stories displayed, maximum 6 on homepage. View all option to expand
**Priority:** High **Effort:** Medium

### Task 8: Which Bean Are You — Quiz Page (New Page)

- Create new standalone page — URL: beanidentity.com/which-bean-are-you
- Page heading — 'Which Bean Are You?'
- Sub-heading — 'Answer 5 questions. Meet your Bean.'
- Quiz structure — will be shared with you
- Results page — shows the bean character they matched with, character description, traits, and a
    direct CTA to shop that bean's collection
- Results must be shareable — generate a shareable image/card for Instagram Stories
- Quiz completion to trigger email capture — 'Save your result + get early access to drops' with
    email input
- Quiz results data to feed into email segmentation — tag subscribers by their bean type
- Link in bio on Instagram to point directly to this page
- Page to be mobile-optimised as primary traffic will come from Instagram
**Priority:** High **Effort:** High

### Task 9: Bean Character Pages Ref : https://www.lush.com/

_Currently clicking a bean in the navigation takes the visitor straight to products with zero context about who
that bean is. Each bean needs a dedicated landing page that introduces the character before showing the
products. The product should be the last thing on the page, not the first._

- Redesign all 5 bean collection pages: Coffee Bean, Chilli Bean, Vanilla Bean, Jelly Bean, Green
    Bean
- Each page to open with full-width character banner — bean illustration, character name, archetype
    title
- Below banner — Character Introduction block with: Nickname, Core personality description, 4-
    key traits, Voice and tone, Audience that relates
- After character introduction — product grid (existing functionality retained)
- Add 'Are you a [Bean]?' CTA linking to the quiz page above the product grid
- Each page to have a distinct colour accent matching the bean — no generic white pages
- Fix typo on Coffee Bean collection page — URL currently reads 'coffe-bean', needs to be
    corrected to 'coffee-bean' with a 301 redirect from old URL
- Bean character pages to be linked from the quiz results page
**Priority:** High **Effort:** High


### Task 10: Navigation Typo Fix — Immediate Action

```
The navigation currently reads 'Coffe Bean' instead of 'Coffee Bean'. This is a small error with
disproportionate impact — it signals an unpolished brand to every first-time visitor and erodes trust before
they even see a product.
```
- Fix navigation label from 'Coffe Bean' to 'Coffee Bean'
- Audit all other instances of bean names across the site for consistency in capitalisation and
    spelling
- Ensure all five bean names are consistently formatted: Coffee Bean, Chilli Bean, Vanilla Bean,
    Jelly Bean, Green Bean
- This task should be completed within 24 hours of SOW sign-off — it is not dependent on any other
    task
**Priority:** High **Effort:** Low

## 04 OUT OF SCOPE

The following items are explicitly not included in this engagement and should not be actioned without a
separate brief and sign-off:

- Any changes to the product pages beyond the bean character page introduction block
- Redesign of the checkout flow
- Integration of a new payment gateway
- SEO optimisation or meta tag changes
- Blog page redesign
- Any backend logic changes to Shopify inventory or fulfilment

## 06 IMPORTANT NOTES & ASSUMPTIONS

- All development is on the existing Shopify store. No platform migration is included in this scope.
- The quiz requires a quiz app or custom Shopify section. Developer to recommend preferred
    approach and flag any app costs to client before proceeding.
- Task 7 (typo fix) is independent of all other tasks and should be completed immediately upon
    SOW sign-off without waiting for assets or approvals.
- Mobile-first design is non-negotiable — the primary audience accesses the site via Instagram on
    mobile.
- Any third-party app costs (quiz app, email pop-up app, marquee app) to be confirmed and
    approved by client before installation.
