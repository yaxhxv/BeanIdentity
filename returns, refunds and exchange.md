# RETURN, REFUND & EXCHANGE POLICY

This Return, Refund & Exchange Policy (“Policy”) governs the sale of products by **Bean Identity** (“we”,
“us”, “our”) through our website and applies to all customers placing orders with us.

By placing an order, you agree to be bound by the terms set out below.

**1. NATURE OF PRODUCTS (PRINT-ON-DEMAND)**

All Bean Identity products are made-to-order and printed only after an order is placed. As these
products are produced specifically for each customer, returns and exchanges are limited.

**2. ELIGIBILITY FOR RETURNS OR REPLACEMENTS**

We accept returns or provide replacements only under the following circumstances:

- Product received is damaged
- Product has a manufacturing or printing defect
- Incorrect product (wrong design, color, or size) was delivered

Requests must be raised within **5 (Five) days** from the date of delivery.

**3. NON-ELIGIBLE CASES**

Returns, refunds, or exchanges will **not** be accepted in the following situations:

- Change of mind or personal preference
- Color or print variation due to screen/display differences
- Minor variations in print placement within industry-accepted tolerance
- Requests raised after the 5-day reporting window

Refunds will not be provided for size-related concerns where the correct size ordered by the
customer was produced and delivered.

**4. SIZE EXCHANGE POLICY**

Customers are strongly advised to review the size chart provided on the product page before placing
an order.

However, Bean Identity understands that sizing concerns may occasionally occur. Therefore, we may
allow a one-time size exchange under the following conditions:

- Exchange request must be raised within **5 (five ) days** from the date of delivery
- Product must be:
    o unused
    o unwashed
    o undamaged

**6. RETURN & REPLACEMENT REQUEST PROCESS**

To initiate a return or replacement request, customers must contact us within **5 days of delivery** at:

Email: support@beanidentity.com

Subject Line: **Return / Replacement Request – Order #[Order ID]**

The request must include:

- Order ID
- Clear images or video showing the issue
- A brief description of the problem
**7. VERIFICATION & RESOLUTION**

All requests are subject to verification by our support team.

- Approved claims will be eligible for a free replacement at no additional cost.
- Approved size exchange requests will be processed subject to product availability.
- For defective or damaged products, physical return of the item may not be required.
    However, for approved size exchanges, customers may be required to return the original
    product before a replacement is processed.
- If replacement is not feasible, a refund may be issued at our discretion.

Refunds will be processed to the original payment method used during purchase and may take **5 –
business days** to reflect depending on the payment provider.

**8. SHIPPING CHARGES**

Shipping charges are **non-refundable** , except in cases where the product received is defective,
damaged, or incorrect.
